# Cart rules (prototype)

1. Fulfillment: Delivery from local store only. Skip anything that is shipping-only or tagged "Separate delivery".
2. Pick the cheapest by unit price (per oz, per lb, or per count) among in-stock delivery items.
3. Single pack only. Skip multi-packs ("(12 pack)", "2 Pack", "Case of"). When a card offers Single / N Pack options, choose Single and compare on the Single unit price.
4. Package size cap: skip packages over 5 lb / 80 oz unless the item is normally bought bulk (rice is OK up to 20 lb).
5. Quantity: 1 unless the list says otherwise.
6. Match the plain meaning of the list item. "chicken breast" means raw boneless skinless breast, not tenders, nuggets, or canned.
7. If no clear match, skip and report instead of guessing.
